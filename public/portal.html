⚖️ Executive Answers to Chairman Bowdre's Inquiries

Municipal Solutions
Q1: How do we prevent County balance sheet risk & volatility?
Answer: The Tripartite Custodial Model ensures Miami-Dade County never holds cryptocurrency or private keys. USTIB (USVI DBIR Bank) handles all USD clearing and statutory trusts, while BitGo (OCC National Trust) provides qualified digital asset key custody.
Q2: How do we protect resident privacy without database honeypots?
Answer: Zero cleartext PII touches the blockchain. C-nodes compute client-side Groth16 zk-SNARK mathematical proofs on-device. G-codes verify eligibility (e.g. resident age or district) with zero data leakage.
Q3: How do we insulate County IT from external cyber threats?
Answer: Design Law 1 (The One-Way Wall) strictly isolates the public civic edge from internal county origin databases. Public kiosks read cryptographic attestations but possess zero write access to internal municipal systems.
Q4: How do contractors receive programmatic milestone payments?
Answer: The automated AIA Document G702/G703 escrow engine releases funds automatically under 2-of-3 multi-sig consensus upon certified licensed engineering inspection sign-off.
