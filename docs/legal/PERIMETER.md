# ⚖️ MIA BY VIA — STRUCTURAL BOUNDARIES & TRIPWIRE ESCALATION POLICIES

## 1. Structural Boundaries & Non-Custodial Limits

MIA by VIA acts as an engineering control layer and technical infrastructure provider. It does **not** act as:

1. A direct lender, bank, or financial custodian.
2. A licensed transfer agent or municipal debt issuer.
3. A public PII repository or biometric data holder.

---

## 2. Tripwires & Automated Gate Freezes

**Tripwires:** Any attempt to introduce tokenized municipal debt, direct fiat disbursement flows without a licensed banking partner, or unencrypted PII logging on public ledgers triggers an immediate ANVIL G-Gate freeze and mandatory legal review.
